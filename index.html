Questions

Before you ask.

Do I need to be on camera?

Yes. Patients trust the doctor they will actually see. You record short raw clips on your phone. We send the topics, then edit, caption and post. If footage misses the monthly date, that month's reels move with it.

Who will the WhatsApp assistant message?

Only your existing patients who have agreed to receive messages from your clinic. That means appointment reminders, follow-ups and recalls. You confirm that consent is in place before we switch it on.

Do you guarantee rankings or new patients?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. We set your clinic up to be found on Google and in AI search and keep it updated. Your monthly report shows what changed.

What does "AI search" mean here?

People increasingly ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI answers to recommend a clinic. We make sure the information those tools draw on about your clinic is complete, accurate and easy for them to read.

Which clinics do you work with?

Dermatology, dental, IVF, hair transplant and cosmetic clinics, starting in Indore. We do not take weight-loss clinics.

What is the commitment?

Three months on any monthly plan, then month to month. The ₹17,000 setup is credited against your first month if you start a plan within 30 days.
